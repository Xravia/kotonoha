# Kotonoha 言の葉

Learn Japanese from your own songs and episodes. This is the installable phone version of Kotonoha: open the page once in Chrome, then choose **Install app** from the menu. It works offline afterwards.

- Add songs or episodes from the phone (Library > Choose songs). Lyrics come from a same-name .lrc/.srt/.ass/.vtt file, or from LRCLIB (lrclib.net) when you ask for them.
- Each song becomes a lesson: Watch, Learn, Play and Test, plus a word garden for review and kanji writing practice.
- Everything you add and your progress stay on the device. Nothing is uploaded.
- Optional local helper (Settings > Local helper): a small AI that runs on the phone's graphics chip through WebGPU and adds translations, notes, recaps and grading. It downloads once (about 1 GB for the small model) from Hugging Face.

Data: JMdict and KANJIDIC2 (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0), kuromoji.js (Apache 2.0) with IPADIC (NAIST), JLPT lists by Jonathan Waller (CC BY), KanjiVG by Ulrich Apel (CC BY-SA 3.0), WebLLM by MLC AI (Apache 2.0). Details in data/LICENSES.txt.
